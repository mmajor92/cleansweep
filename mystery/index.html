How to play

  1. A mystery thing is hidden in the list. Tap one to guess it, or type to find it (you can skip a, an and the).
  2. Each guess shows how its numbers compare with the mystery thing’s. An arrow points the way: up means higher or later, down means lower or earlier. Easy and Medium add a colour for how close you are: green is within double or half, red is further off.
  3. Use the clues to rule things out. You have six guesses. Closer guesses, and fewer of them, score more.

Size is the thing’s main measurement: a tower’s height, a ship’s length, a ball’s width. Year is when it was built, launched or happened.

Easy shrinks the list for you as you guess. Medium doesn’t. Hard doesn’t either, and takes the colours away too. You get one try a day: your setting locks when you make your first guess. Practice puzzles are unlimited.

Want to try one first? There are two practice puzzles. They are the same for everyone, you can play them as often as you like, and they do not count towards your score.
