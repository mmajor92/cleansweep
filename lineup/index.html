How to play

  1. Put the six things in order, as it says above them. Tap one thing and then another to swap them, or drag one.
  2. Press Check order. Anything in the right place turns green and locks. Then rearrange the rest.
  3. You have three tries. Fewer tries is better.

Each puzzle is one kind of measure: size (a length or a height), weight, speed or year.

How close together the six things are changes from day to day. You get one puzzle a day.

Points. Each thing scores for the try it was right on (100, 60, then 35), with a little for a near miss. A clean sweep adds 100. Hard days count more.
