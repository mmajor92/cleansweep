How to play

  1. Is the next thing higher or lower than the last one? Tap your answer.
  2. The true value is revealed and becomes your new starting point. Now and then the same object carries you into a different kind of measure.
  3. Seven questions. Get them all right for a Clean Sweep.

Want to try one first? There are two practice puzzles. They are the same for everyone, you can play them as often as you like, and they do not count towards your streak.
